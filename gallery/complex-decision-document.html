§1 Executive Summary

NovaPay processes $48M/month across 6 corridors (US, UK, EU, Canada, Singapore, Australia). Our current single-provider arrangement with LegacyPay expires Q3 2026, creating a 12-week window to migrate. Three candidates emerged from RFP: ClearBridge Payments (established US/UK specialist), MeridianPay (EU-native SEPA/PSD2 expert), and ApexTransact (newer entrant claiming global coverage at aggressive pricing).

§1.1Background & Constraints

NovaPay’s platform enables cross-border payroll disbursements for 340+ employer clients across 12 countries. Settlement timing directly impacts employee satisfaction — a 24-hour delay in any corridor triggers SLA penalties.

Hard constraints:

  • Settlement within T+1 for US/UK, T+2 for EU corridors
  • SOC 2 Type II and PCI DSS Level 1 compliance
  • Monthly transaction cost < $180K at current volume
  • Webhook-first integration (no polling)

Soft constraints:

  • Prefer REST over SOAP APIs
  • Multi-currency settlement without manual FX conversion
  • Sandbox environment for pre-production testing
§1.2Evaluation Methodology

Each provider was evaluated across 4 dimensions: coverage (jurisdictions & payment rails), economics (per-transaction + fixed costs), compliance (certifications & audit posture), and integration complexity (API maturity, webhook reliability, sandbox quality). Scores were weighted: coverage 35%, economics 25%, compliance 25%, integration 15%.

§2 Provider Options

Three providers emerged from NovaPay’s RFP process. Each occupies a distinct position: ClearBridge dominates US/UK corridors, MeridianPay owns EU/SEPA, and ApexTransact promises global coverage at aggressive pricing. The evaluation below surfaces where documented capability ends and vendor claims begin.

ClearBridge Payments Recommended

Production

Established US/UK payment processor. 8 years in production, 1,200+ enterprise clients. SOC 2 Type II certified since 2021. REST-first API with webhook delivery and idempotency keys on all mutation endpoints.

§2.1aCoverage

US: ACH (same-day and next-day), Fedwire for urgent transfers. UK: Faster Payments (instant, £1M limit), BACS for batch. Canada: EFT via partnership with Canadian processor ◔ Verbal.

Gap: No EU/SEPA capability. No APAC coverage. Singapore and Australia corridors would require a second provider.

§2.1bPricing

$0.25/transaction (ACH), $0.40/transaction (FPS). FX spread: 0.15% on GBP/USD conversions. No monthly minimums. Volume discount at 500K+ txns/month: 15% reduction ✓ Confirmed.

§2.1cCompliance

SOC 2 Type II (annual renewal, last audit March 2026). PCI DSS Level 1. GDPR data processing agreement available. US FinCEN MSB registration active. UK FCA authorization for payment services ✓ Confirmed.

MeridianPay Alternative

Production

EU-native payment processor built on SEPA infrastructure. PSD2 licensed. Strong EUR settlement with SEPA Instant support. 5 years in production, 400+ clients primarily in European fintech. REST API with SOAP fallback for legacy SEPA Credit Transfer rails.

§2.2aCoverage

EU (36 SEPA countries): SEPA Credit Transfer, SEPA Instant, SEPA Direct Debit. Coverage across all Eurozone + EEA countries ✓ Confirmed.

UK: GBP payments via correspondent banking — not native FPS. Settlement T+2 for GBP ◔ Verbal. Gap: No US, Canada, or APAC coverage.

§2.2bPricing

€0.10/transaction (SEPA SCT), €0.35/transaction (SEPA Instant). No FX spread on EUR-to-EUR. Cross-currency: 0.25% spread + €2.00 wire fee for non-EUR destinations. Monthly platform fee: €500. Volume discount at 200K+ txns: 20% reduction ✓ Confirmed.

§2.2cCompliance

PSD2 licensed (Netherlands DNB). SOC 2 Type II. GDPR native — all data stored in EU (Frankfurt, Dublin). PCI DSS Level 2. ✓ Confirmed. Note: PCI Level 2 (not Level 1) — sufficient for our volume but worth noting for future scale.

ApexTransact Caution

Evaluating

Newer entrant (founded 2023) claiming coverage across 12 corridors with a single API. Aggressive pricing at $0.15/transaction flat. SOC 2 Type I only — Type II “expected Q4 2026” ⏳ Coming Soon. Limited production references; largest disclosed client processes $5M/month.

Risk: Dashed lines above indicate corridors supported only by verbal claims or “coming soon” roadmap items. Only US/UK/CA rails have been independently verified via sandbox testing.
§2.3aCoverage

Verified (sandbox tested): US ACH, UK FPS, Canada EFT ✓ Confirmed.

Claimed (verbal only): EU SEPA, Singapore FAST, Australia NPP ◔ Verbal. Sales rep stated “full production by Q3 2026” but no documentation exists.

Roadmap: Mexico SPEI, Brazil PIX, India UPI ⏳ Coming Soon. “On the roadmap for 2027” — no API documentation or timeline commitment.

§2.3bPricing

$0.15/transaction flat across all corridors. FX spread: 0.10% ◔ Verbal — not in the written proposal. No monthly minimum. Enterprise pricing “available” but no published tiers.

The $0.15 flat rate is 40% below ClearBridge’s US ACH rate. At ApexTransact’s current disclosed volume ($5M/month largest client), this pricing may not survive scale — renegotiation risk is material.
§2.3cCompliance

SOC 2 Type I (completed January 2026). Type II “in progress, expected Q4 2026” ⏳ Coming Soon. PCI DSS Level 1 — passed April 2026 ✓ Confirmed. No GDPR DPA available yet. No EU data residency option.

SOC 2 Type I without Type II means the audit assessed design of controls, not operating effectiveness over time. This is a compliance gap for NovaPay’s own SOC 2 report, which requires Type II from critical subprocessors.

§8.3 Recommendation

Recommended: Dual-Provider (ClearBridge + MeridianPay)

Adopt ClearBridge for US/UK/CA corridors and MeridianPay for EU/SEPA. This covers 5 of 6 active corridors with confirmed, production-tested capabilities. Canada EFT coverage pending ClearBridge written confirmation (Q1 above).

Defer ApexTransact until SOC 2 Type II is achieved and APAC coverage can be independently verified. Re-evaluate for Singapore/Australia expansion in Q1 2027.

Estimated annual cost: $1.92M (vs. $2.16M current LegacyPay) — 11% reduction with faster settlement and better coverage.
